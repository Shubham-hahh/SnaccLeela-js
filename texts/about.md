# About Crossie

Hello! I'm **Crossie** — an independent game developer, interactive designer, researcher, and explorer of the unified continuum between scientific rigor and spiritual realization. 

My life and work operate at the precise intersection of **technical precision, cosmic curiosity, and existential depth**. For me, code, mechanics, and 3D graphics are not merely functional tools; they are mediums of spiritual expression, consciousness modeling, and universe-building.

---

## 🌌 The Core Spectrum: Scientific Spiritualism

I do not view science and spirituality as opposing forces, but as twin languages attempting to decode the same ultimate reality:
* **Science** provides the methodology, empirical rigor, mathematical harmony, and observable mechanics of existence.
* **Spirituality** provides the introspective depth, awareness, purpose, inner alchemy, and felt resonance of being.

To build a world—whether through code, atmospheric physics, or story—is to engage in a micro-cosmic act of creation. Science gives the creation its laws; spiritualism gives it its soul.
